## Critical Listening and Immersive Experience

My critical-listening development includes a quantified public SoundGym training record with an SPI of 3,410,130 and SoundGym’s Diamond Ears Award. Because SoundGym calculates SPI from the three highest scores in each audio game, the record provides standardized supporting evidence across frequency, dynamics, level, panning, and spatial-listening exercises rather than merely documenting activity volume. It is not presented as an unsupported percentile or as standalone proof of complete professional tuning proficiency.

I later studied Jason Corey’s technical-ear-training methodology in an academic course. My continued execution now contains 693 documented trials: 372 pink-noise EQ trials with criterion-specific cumulative accuracy from 80–90%, approximately 301 hard-clipping trials with a best exercise result of 88%, and 20 early bit-depth-reduction trials at 47%. Compression and reverberation are labeled exploratory, while Mid/Side, expansion, and editing remain unscored. These results are limited to the sources and settings tested, and the SoundGym and Corey records remain separate forms of evidence because their tasks and scoring systems are not directly comparable.

Relevant immersive experience includes professional work at Ocean Way Nashville and Starstruck Studios, introductory studio-maintenance instruction from Mike Porter, guided work in 7.1.4 Dolby Atmos environments, an Ocean Way immersive-recording independent study, and participation as a listener—not an author—in three published AES studies. Together, this supports continued development as a trained critical listener for room-acoustic, loudspeaker, electroacoustic, spatial-audio, and automotive applications.
