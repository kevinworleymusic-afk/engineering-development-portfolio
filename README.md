## Critical Listening and Immersive Experience

My critical-listening development includes a **quantified public [SoundGym training record](https://www.soundgym.co/member/profile?m=byy1s26wj7u)** with an SPI of **3,410,130** and SoundGym's **Diamond Ears Award**. Because [SoundGym calculates SPI from the three highest scores in each audio game](https://www.soundgym.co/help/item?id=how-is-spi-calculated), this record provides standardized evidence across frequency, dynamics, level, panning, and spatial-listening exercises rather than merely documenting activity volume. It supports an established critical-listening foundation, but is not presented as an unsupported percentile or as standalone proof of complete professional tuning proficiency. I later studied [Jason Corey's technical-ear-training methodology](https://sites.google.com/a/umich.edu/jason-corey/technical-ear-training) in an academic course, and my [Technical Ear Training repository](https://github.com/kevinworleymusic-afk/technical-ear-training) documents continued Corey-based development through repeatable chapter exercises, baseline testing, progress tracking, and technical reflection. The SoundGym and Corey results remain separate forms of evidence because their tasks and scoring systems are not directly comparable. My full linked background is available in [Prior Listening and Ear-Training Experience](https://github.com/kevinworleymusic-afk/technical-ear-training/blob/main/PRIOR_EXPERIENCE.md).

Relevant experience includes professional work at [Ocean Way Nashville](https://oceanwaynashville.com/) and [Starstruck Studios](https://www.starstruckentertainment.com/studios); introductory studio-maintenance instruction from [Mike Porter](https://www.namm.org/library/oral-history/mike-porter/); guided work in 7.1.4 Dolby Atmos environments; the [Ocean Way immersive-recording independent study](Projects/Technical-Audio-and-Research/Ocean-Way-Jazz-Combo-Dolby-Atmos-Independent-Study/README.md); and participation as a listener—not an author—in the published AES studies [*Binaural Renderers Accuracy Comparison: Part I*](https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=22327), [*Limits of the Cochlear Bandpass*](https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=22317), and [*Detection of Phase Alignment and Polarity in Drum Tracks*](https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=22248).
