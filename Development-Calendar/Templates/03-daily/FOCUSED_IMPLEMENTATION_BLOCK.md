## Immediate-Start Checklist

- [ ] Required files, tools, references, or contact context are open.
- [ ] The current state and previous next step are understood.
- [ ] Today’s output is named before work begins.
- [ ] Evidence will be saved or linked.
- [ ] Work outside the stated scope will be deferred.

## Stop Conditions

End the block when:

- The stated output or decision is complete.
- Enough evidence exists to select the next step.
- A dependency or blocker prevents valid progress.
- Continued work would expand beyond the selected scope.
- Attention or work quality has declined enough to make further output unreliable.
