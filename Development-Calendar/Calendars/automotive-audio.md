I used that information to refine career targeting, technical-development priorities, project feasibility, roadmap sequencing, and a tabulated set of portfolio ideas.

The calendar records the resulting information gathering and my independent synthesis. It does not imply formal supervision, endorsement, collaboration, or co-authorship by the people named.
