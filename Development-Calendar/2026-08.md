# August 2026 Engineering Development Plan

[Master calendar](README.md) · [Main portfolio](../README.md) · [Critical-listening calendar](https://github.com/kevinworleymusic-afk/technical-ear-training/tree/main/calendar)

## Month Objective

Convert the portfolio foundation into coordinated, evidence-producing work across critical listening, acoustics and measurement, automotive audio, and DSP/software. Individual weekly and daily tasks should remain realistic and should link to evidence rather than becoming a second copy of each project repository.

## August 1–2

**Progression marker:** Complete calendar integration and establish the automotive-employer research base.

On August 1, review current automotive-audio openings and create a target-company list containing relevant locations, industry roles, and representative position types. Distinguish active openings from longer-term targets. Also ask Alek Weidman for recommended electrical-engineering self-study materials or a learning sequence that could build on the introductory studio-electronics course completed with Mike Porter. Use the master calendar as the overview and preserve detailed listening plans in the [Technical Ear Training repository](https://github.com/kevinworleymusic-afk/technical-ear-training).

## August 3–9

**Progression marker:** Begin a balanced cross-disciplinary cycle.

Select:

- one critical-listening focus;
- one engineering or acoustics study focus;
- one software, DSP, or automotive portfolio action.

The objective is execution and documentation, not equal time in every category.

## August 10–16

**Progression marker:** Stabilize selected development priorities.

Continue the areas that produced useful evidence during the prior week. Keep maintenance work brief and give the primary development block to the area needing deeper competency.

## August 17–23

**Progression marker:** Advance difficulty or project scope from evidence.

Increase one variable at a time: listening difficulty, analytical depth, software functionality, experimental specificity, or automotive-system detail.

## August 24–30

**Progression marker:** Produce or consolidate reviewable evidence.

Prioritize an artifact an employer can inspect: a scored listening record, concise technical note, plot, validated software behavior, project diagram, methodology update, or documented design decision.

## August 31

**Progression marker:** Review August and establish September priorities.

Record:

- work completed;
- evidence produced;
- competencies maintained or advanced;
- blockers or access requirements;
- the most valuable next monthly objective.

## Planning Rule

The master calendar records concise cross-disciplinary milestones. Detailed methods, exercises, code, data, and limitations remain in their corresponding repositories and are linked from here.
