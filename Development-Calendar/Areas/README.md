## Planning Flow

1. Choose the relevant development categories at the beginning of the week.
2. Define each category's weekly process or objective in its detailed view.
3. Place a brief linked category entry on each scheduled day of the master calendar.
4. Add links to daily logs or completed evidence as the work occurs.
