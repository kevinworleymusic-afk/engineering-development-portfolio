# Engineering Development Area Calendars

[Master calendar](../README.md)

The master calendar contains only short linked category entries. These area calendars contain the fuller weekly plans, category-specific date entries, and links to underlying evidence.

- [Critical Listening calendar gateway](critical-listening.md)
- [Automotive-Audio calendar](automotive-audio.md)
- [DSP calendar](dsp.md)
- [Software-Development calendar](software-development.md)
- [Research & Technical-Study calendar](research-and-technical-study.md)
- [Portfolio & Planning calendar](portfolio-and-planning.md)

## Planning Flow

1. Choose the relevant development categories at the beginning of the week.
2. Define each category's weekly process or objective in its area calendar.
3. Populate the applicable dates in each area calendar.
4. Add only a brief linked category entry to those dates on the master calendar.
5. Replace or supplement plan links with daily logs and completed evidence as the work occurs.

Critical Listening remains linked to the authoritative calendar in the Technical Ear Training repository so its calendar, exercises, chapters, templates, and session logs remain together.
