## Shared Planning Flow

1. Choose the relevant development categories at the beginning of the week.
2. Define each category's weekly process or objective in its detailed calendar.
3. Populate the applicable dates in that calendar.
4. Add only a brief linked category entry to those dates on the master calendar.
5. Replace or supplement plan links with daily logs and completed evidence as work occurs.

Critical Listening links to the authoritative calendar in the Technical Ear Training repository. The other detailed calendars are maintained in this portfolio's [Calendars](../Calendars/) folder.
