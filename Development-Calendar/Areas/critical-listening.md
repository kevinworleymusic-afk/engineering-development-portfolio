# Critical-Listening Calendar Gateway

[Master calendar](../README.md) · [Open the full Critical-Listening Calendar](https://github.com/kevinworleymusic-afk/technical-ear-training/tree/main/calendar)

The authoritative **objective listening practice** calendar remains in the Technical Ear Training repository so its monthly objectives, weekly plans, daily logs, chapter results, warmup procedures, and diagnostic records stay together. Within the broader Critical Listening category, this scored identification/matching practice remains distinct from the Subjective Listening Evaluation System.

## Calendar Flow

1. Set the week's objective listening-practice processes in the [Technical Ear Training calendar](https://github.com/kevinworleymusic-afk/technical-ear-training/tree/main/calendar).
2. Populate its weekly and daily records with the detailed exercises, settings, and targets.
3. Add only a short linked Critical Listening entry to each applicable day on the [master calendar](../README.md).
4. Link completed days to the detailed listening session or calendar entry.
