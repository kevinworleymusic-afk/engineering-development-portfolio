# Acoustical Consulting Development Gateway

[Master calendar](../README.md) · [Open the full Acoustical Consulting Calendar](../Calendars/acoustical-consulting.md)

This pathway tracks preparation for acoustical-consulting work: professional outreach, role and industry research, informational conversations, evidence review, measurement and reporting development, and translation of existing technical-audio experience into consulting-relevant competencies.

It documents development toward consulting work; it does not present preparation or networking as professional consulting experience.

## Calendar Flow

1. Record confirmed calls, outreach, applications, research, and consulting-focused technical development in the [Acoustical Consulting Calendar](../Calendars/acoustical-consulting.md).
2. Use exact dates for confirmed events and clearly label broader research or networking periods.
3. Add only a short linked CONSULT entry to each applicable day on the [master calendar](../README.md).
4. Connect later technical work to evidence such as measurement procedures, reports, calculations, standards study, or client-oriented recommendations.
