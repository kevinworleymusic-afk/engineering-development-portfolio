# Professional Networking & Acoustical Consulting Gateway

[Master calendar](../README.md) · [Open the full Professional Networking & Acoustical Consulting Calendar](../Calendars/acoustical-consulting.md)

This pathway tracks professional relationship-building across acoustical consulting, automotive audio, DSP, critical listening, and related technical-audio work. It includes outreach, informational conversations, role and industry research, follow-up, evidence review, and translation of professional input into development priorities.

Acoustical consulting is a focused part of this broader networking record. The pathway documents career development and professional input; it does not present networking or preparation as professional consulting experience.

## Calendar Flow

1. Record confirmed calls, outreach, applications, research, and consulting-focused technical development in the [Professional Networking & Acoustical Consulting Calendar](../Calendars/acoustical-consulting.md).
2. Use exact dates for confirmed events and clearly label broader research or networking periods.
3. Add only a short linked NET entry to each applicable day on the [master calendar](../README.md).
4. Connect later technical work to evidence such as measurement procedures, reports, calculations, standards study, or client-oriented recommendations.
