# SSL Fusion Audio Precision Measurement Project

## Project Type

AET-5010 **Acoustic & Electroacoustic Measurement and Design** course report completed at Belmont University under Dr. Doyuen Ko and dated April 27, 2023.

## Publication Status

This is an **unpublished academic course project**, not peer-reviewed research or a professional product-certification report. The original report identifies Kevin Worley and documents the work using first-person plural language; this page does not infer unlisted collaborator roles.

## Device Under Test

The device under test was an **SSL Fusion**, an all-analog stereo outboard processor used for color, saturation, depth, compression, distortion, and other mix-bus processing.

## Measurement Scope

The report documents use of an Audio Precision test environment to examine:

- Frequency response
- Total harmonic distortion plus noise (THD+N)
- Phase
- Interchannel crosstalk
- Signal-to-noise/reference-level behavior

The work required configuring analog generator and analyzer functions, selecting frequencies and bandwidths, routing two channels, capturing graphical results, comparing channels, and relating measured results to manufacturer specifications.

## Results Preserved From the Course Report

The source report records:

- Approximately **+17.3 dB** across 20 Hz-20 kHz under the documented frequency-response setup
- **0.00108% THD+N** for channel A and **0.00113% THD+N** for channel B at 1 kHz with a 22 kHz bandwidth
- Phase readings of approximately **+0.02°, +0.08°, and +0.12°** across the three reported test frequencies
- Crosstalk readings of **-47.675 dB** and **-47.791 dB** at 10 kHz
- Analyzer reference readings of **+17.323 dBr** and **+17.390 dBr** in the section labeled signal-to-noise ratio

These values are reproduced as historical course-report evidence and have not been independently recalculated from raw Audio Precision data.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Audio Precision generator/analyzer operation
- Analog stereo device measurement
- Frequency-response sweeps
- THD+N measurement
- Phase and crosstalk evaluation
- Bandwidth and reference-level configuration
- Channel comparison
- Manufacturer-specification review
- Technical reporting with plots and screenshots

## Limitations and Interpretation

The report repeatedly notes that its measurement parameters differed from the manufacturer’s default conditions. The frequency-response, crosstalk, and noise/reference readings therefore should not be treated as direct verification or contradiction of SSL’s published specifications.

Some terminology and units in the original report are internally inconsistent, including references to unity gain, rated power, watts, dBu, dBr, and manufacturer noise specifications. Raw project files and exact Audio Precision setup files are not included, so the results cannot presently be reproduced or independently validated. The value of this artifact is evidence of early hands-on Audio Precision measurement practice and technical reporting, not a claim of a completed professional validation campaign.
