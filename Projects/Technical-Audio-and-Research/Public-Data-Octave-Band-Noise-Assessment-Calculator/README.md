# Public-Data Octave-Band Noise Assessment Calculator

**Status:** Planned project; design published August 1, 2026. The workbook and results are not yet complete.

This planned project will develop and validate a reusable spreadsheet for combining publicly reported octave-band mechanical-equipment sound-power spectra, applying A-weighting, comparing operating scenarios, and identifying dominant sources and frequency bands.

## Project Design

- [Project Design and Reviewer Feedback Brief](Public-Data-Noise-Assessment-Calculator-Project-Design.pdf)

The PDF is intentionally suitable for sending to a knowledgeable friend or practitioner. It includes specific questions about entry-level consulting relevance, scope boundaries, useful outputs, common mistakes, and appropriate next-stage methods.

## Initial Scope

- Public sound-power spectra for a CRAC condenser, VRF condensers, and a toilet extract fan
- Source-quantity adjustment and logarithmic octave-band combination
- Overall unweighted and A-weighted sound-power totals
- Baseline, reduced-operation, isolated-source, and doubled-system scenarios
- Dominant-source and dominant-band analysis
- Known-result validation cases and independent checks

## Boundary

Version 1 will not predict sound pressure at a receiver or claim regulatory compliance. Propagation, barriers, ground effects, reflections, background corrections, room criteria, and vehicle-cabin transfer behavior remain future work requiring defined methods and additional inputs.

## Professional Context

The design responds to professional guidance from Ray Plasse emphasizing practical Excel ability, octave-band levels, A-weighting, decibel summation, and the distinction between sound power and sound pressure. Ray's guidance informed the study direction; it does not imply supervision, review, endorsement, or collaboration.
