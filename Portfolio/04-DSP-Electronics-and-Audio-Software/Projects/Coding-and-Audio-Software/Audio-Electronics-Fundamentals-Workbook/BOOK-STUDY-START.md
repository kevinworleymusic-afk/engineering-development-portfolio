## First Planning Session

Before completing exercises, use one short review session to:

1. Scan the table of contents and major sections.
2. Mark concepts that reinforce prior Mike Porter coursework.
3. Mark concepts that directly support audio electronics, DSP hardware, measurement, troubleshooting, or automotive-audio systems.
4. Compare those topics with the existing Version 1 voltage-divider, loading, and RC-filter plan.
5. Select one small first unit that can be calculated and independently checked.
6. Record which exercises require simulation, components, test equipment, or additional safety guidance.

## Candidate Topic Sequence

The final sequence remains undecided. Likely candidates for evaluation include:

1. Voltage, current, resistance, and power
2. Series/parallel networks and loading
3. Capacitors, inductors, impedance, and RC filters
4. Diodes, transistors, and basic semiconductor behavior
5. Operational-amplifier fundamentals
6. Regulators and power supplies
7. Audio amplifiers, microphones, and loudspeakers
8. Measurement, troubleshooting, and practical construction considerations

Listing a topic here does not mean it has been completed or selected for the final plan.

## Exercise Documentation Template

For each selected concept or exercise, document:

- chapter/section and source;
- learning objective;
- circuit or system context;
- given values and assumptions;
- hand calculation;
- spreadsheet or code-based calculation;
- simulator result, when practical;
- hardware measurement, only when equipment and safety requirements are satisfied;
- comparison among expected and observed results;
- audio or automotive relevance;
- errors, limitations, and next step.
