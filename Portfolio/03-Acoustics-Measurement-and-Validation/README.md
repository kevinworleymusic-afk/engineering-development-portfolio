# Acoustics, Measurement & Validation

**Acoustical Analysis · Audio Measurement · Signal Analysis · Verification · Technical Reporting**

My measurement and acoustics work combines graduate audio-engineering training with **Audio Precision measurement, room and signal analysis, MATLAB/Python processing, environmental-noise modeling, controlled comparison, validation planning, and technical reporting**.

The common thread is disciplined evidence: define the condition, measure or model it appropriately, understand what the method can and cannot establish, document assumptions and limitations, and identify the next test required for a stronger conclusion.

This page is my targeted portfolio for **acoustics, electroacoustics, test, measurement, validation, and engineering-analysis roles**.

## Measurement Philosophy

I do not treat a graph or single-number metric as the conclusion by itself. A useful engineering result also requires knowing **how the stimulus was generated, what reference condition was used, what the instrument or method actually measures, what uncertainty or confounds remain, and what decision the evidence supports**.

That mindset is especially important in audio, where objective system behavior and listener perception must ultimately be reconciled rather than optimized independently.
