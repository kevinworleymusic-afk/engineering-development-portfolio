## Measurement philosophy

The goal of this work is not simply to produce plots or single-number results. I aim to document stimulus and reference conditions, understand instrument and method limitations, separate observation from interpretation, preserve repeatability, and identify what additional measurement would be needed before making a stronger conclusion.
