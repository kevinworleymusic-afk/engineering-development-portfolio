# Employer Master List — Technical Audio, Acoustical Consulting, and Georgia Music Education

This employer map now contains three separate research and application systems: **automotive audio**, **acoustical consulting**, and **Georgia music education**. Each discipline has its own dashboard and tracker so different hiring structures, credentials, time horizons, and priorities are not blurred together.

The automotive-audio side organizes the ecosystem into seven practical lanes:

1. Tier 1 and integrated-system suppliers
2. OEM and vehicle manufacturers
3. DSP, audio-software and semiconductor companies
4. Audio-system and component suppliers
5. Measurement, NVH and validation companies
6. Engineering-service and consulting firms
7. Aftermarket and specialty-audio manufacturers

## Acoustical-consulting dashboard

The consulting tracker is divided into four lanes:

1. Southeast and regional practices
2. National specialty-acoustics firms
3. Multidisciplinary AEC and engineering firms
4. Environmental and industrial-noise consultancies

The strongest immediate Atlanta-area targets are **Newcomb & Boyd** and **Waveguide**, followed by local or regional searches at **WSP, Coffman Engineers, AECOM, HDR, Jacobs, Trinity Consultants, NV5, and Salas O’Brien**. Consulting searches should include `acoustical consultant`, `acoustic designer`, `building acoustics`, `noise and vibration`, `environmental noise`, `sound isolation`, `field measurement`, `acoustical modeling`, `AV consultant`, and `commissioning`.

## Georgia music-education dashboard

The music-education view prioritizes Cobb County, Marietta City, Fulton County, Atlanta Public Schools, DeKalb County, City Schools of Decatur, Cherokee County, Paulding County, Douglas County, Gwinnett County, Fayette County, and other systems generally within or near an hour of Atlanta. Because traffic and district geography vary substantially, each school location must be evaluated separately.

The current-leads sheet separates time-sensitive openings from the durable district watchlist. It includes live or recently indexed leads in Fulton, Henry, Atlanta, DeKalb, Decatur, Butts, Gainesville City, and Troup systems, with official or best-available search links and explicit verification notes.

The certification sheet treats **Music P-12 renewal/reactivation as the immediate priority**. Computer Science, CTAE Audio/Video Technology & Film, ESOL, Special Education, Mathematics 6-12, and Elementary Education P-5 are listed only as possibilities requiring a real employment rationale and individual GaPSC verification—not as commitments or confirmed test-only pathways.

The resource section incorporates the user-supplied **GMEA Music Program Leaders Canva site**, its linked hiring-process presentation, GMEA Job Listings, TeachGeorgia, GaDOE Fine Arts resources, GaPSC, and MyPSC.

## Search rule

An employer’s presence in the workbook means it is relevant to the target field—not that it currently has an appropriate opening. Current roles should be recorded only after the posting has been read and its title, location, URL and check date have been saved.

Useful searches include `audio systems`, `acoustic systems`, `vehicle audio`, `audio validation`, `acoustic tuning`, `DSP`, `embedded audio`, `infotainment`, `sound quality`, `NVH`, `electroacoustic`, `applications engineer` and `systems integration`.
