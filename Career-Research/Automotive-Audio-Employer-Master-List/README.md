# Automotive Audio Employer Master List

This employer map organizes the automotive-audio hiring ecosystem into seven practical lanes rather than treating every company as the same kind of target:

1. Tier 1 and integrated-system suppliers
2. OEM and vehicle manufacturers
3. DSP, audio-software and semiconductor companies
4. Audio-system and component suppliers
5. Measurement, NVH and validation companies
6. Engineering-service and consulting firms
7. Aftermarket and specialty-audio manufacturers

The workbook contains **55 initial employers**, likely role families, North American engineering hubs, Southeast relevance, Kevin-specific fit notes, career and evidence links, verification status, and editable application-tracking fields.

## Highest-priority starting groups

- **Regional:** Panasonic Automotive in Peachtree City; Mercedes-Benz in Atlanta; Hyundai/Kia and the Georgia automotive ecosystem; BMW in South Carolina; Volkswagen in Tennessee; JL Audio/Garmin in Florida and North Carolina.
- **Detroit automotive-audio core:** HARMAN, Alps Alpine, FORVIA/Clarion, Bose Automotive, HEAD acoustics, General Motors, Ford and Stellantis.
- **DSP and embedded audio:** DSP Concepts, Dirac, Dolby, Analog Devices, NXP and Texas Instruments.
- **Measurement and validation:** HEAD acoustics, Audio Precision, HBK, GRAS and Klippel.

## Search rule

An employer’s presence in the workbook means it is relevant to the target field—not that it currently has an appropriate opening. Current roles should be recorded only after the posting has been read and its title, location, URL and check date have been saved.

Useful searches include `audio systems`, `acoustic systems`, `vehicle audio`, `audio validation`, `acoustic tuning`, `DSP`, `embedded audio`, `infotainment`, `sound quality`, `NVH`, `electroacoustic`, `applications engineer` and `systems integration`.
